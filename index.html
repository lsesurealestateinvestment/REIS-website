ABOUT THE SOCIETY

Real estate is more
than buildings.

It's about capital, conviction and the decisions that shape the built environment.

The LSESU Real Estate Investment Society (REIS) is a student-led community dedicated to exploring real estate as an investment asset class. We connect academic curiosity with practical insight through research, technical learning and engagement with industry professionals.

What we do ↗

OUR FOUR PILLARS

Built around opportunity.

Four complementary ways to explore the investment industry and develop real-world perspectives.

01 / EXPLORE↗

Industry

RESEARCH AT REIS

Ideas grounded
in analysis.

Our research teams investigate how changing markets, regulation and capital conditions influence investment decisions across property sectors.

01
SECTOR RESEARCH

Commercial & Office

Occupier demand, leasing, sustainability, repositioning and investment performance.

02
SECTOR RESEARCH

Residential

Housing investment, development feasibility, rental markets and changing regulation.

03
SECTOR RESEARCH

Industrial

Logistics, data centres, infrastructure, occupier trends and industrial asset values.
