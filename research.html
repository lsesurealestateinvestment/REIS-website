REIS / INVESTMENT RESEARCH

Research with perspective.

Exploring the markets, assets and decisions shaping real estate investment. Our student-led divisions connect market evidence with investment implications.

THREE SECTORS ONE INVESTMENT LENS

OUR APPROACH

Beyond the headline.

Real estate research is about understanding what drives value—and what could change it.

Across three specialist divisions, our researchers examine fundamentals, capital markets, regulation and structural shifts, with a focus on how these forces influence risk, return and investment decisions.

RESEARCH DIVISIONS

Three sectors.
Distinct perspectives.

Each division is led by a student research head and will publish work as it becomes available.

01 / COMMERCIAL & OFFICE

Commercial & Office.

We explore the forces influencing office and commercial asset performance, from occupier demand and leasing fundamentals to capital values and the economics of refurbishment.

02 / RESIDENTIAL

Residential Research.

Our residential division studies housing market fundamentals, rental dynamics and investment models, considering how affordability, supply and policy affect performance and opportunities.

Industrial Research.

We investigate logistics and industrial real estate, including changing supply chains, occupier requirements and the role of emerging asset types such as data centres.
