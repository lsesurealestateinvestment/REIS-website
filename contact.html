The LSESU Real Estate Investment Society is open to collaborating with real estate professionals, firms and alumni who share an interest in developing the next generation of industry talent. We would welcome conversations around initiatives such as:

01 / INSIGHTS

Share professional experiences and perspectives on real estate investment, market trends and career pathways.

02 / CONNECTIONS

Industry & Career Panels

Help students explore different roles and approaches across the real estate investment industry.

03 / LEARNING

Workshops & Case Discussions

Engage students through practical discussions of investment analysis, valuation and industry decision-making.
